# Divider — Tokens

- Border Default `#e8e8e8` is the subtle rule.
- Border Strong `#a1a1a1` is the strong rule.
- Border Thin is the 1px line thickness.
- Space 1 and Space 2 map to 4px and 8px perpendicular spacing.
- Body font and text-secondary token style the visible label.
- No brand accent is used for a neutral content separator.
