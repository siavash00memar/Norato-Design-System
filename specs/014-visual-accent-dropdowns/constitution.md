# Visual Accent and Dropdown Consistency — Constitution

- Norato yellow is the primary visual accent for selected, checked, active, progress and primary control states.
- Keep blue for links, informational content and explicit info/status semantics; do not erase the secondary brand palette.
- Use shared Norato dropdown visuals and interaction, with RTL and keyboard support; product controls must not open browser-native option menus.
- Preserve Vue 3 APIs, semantic tokens, focus visibility and 48px interaction targets.
