# Stepper — Analyze

The contract describes a progress indicator for sequential work, not a tab/navigation widget. Native ordered-list semantics therefore fit the information model better than Reka ToggleGroup/Tabs or clickable buttons. The controlled current step remains owned by the host so business validation can complete before progress changes. Status is derived from its stable id; explicit error styling does not hide which step is current. A polite live region supplies the required “step N of M” announcement without giving decorative steps keyboard focus. Four visual modes share the same status model, avoiding divergent behavior between variants. Horizontal content will use internal scrolling under narrow widths rather than shrinking labels or stretching the step indicators. design reference's screenshot/contract is the visual comparator, while colors and typography remain Norato tokens.
