# Badge — Constitution

Badge is a compact, non-interactive marker anchored to an icon or avatar. The anchor owns interaction and focus; the Badge must not steal clicks, add a focus ring, or stretch its anchor. It has Count and Dot types, Small and Medium sizes, and Danger, Inverse, Neutral, and Success tones (16 visual combinations). A 2px surface-colored ring separates it from its anchor. In RTL, it sits at the physical top-left corner. Norato semantic tokens and yellow brand identity remain authoritative.
