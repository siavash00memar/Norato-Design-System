# Constitution — collapsed Sidebar current location

- Preserve the exact current page and its semantics in expanded RTL navigation.
- When the current child link is hidden by collapse, retain one visible, accessible indication of its current section.
- Keep the change inside the Sidebar adapter; preserve its API, 48px targets, Norato yellow state treatment, and mobile drawer behavior.
- Do not expose more than one `aria-current` item in the related navigation set.
