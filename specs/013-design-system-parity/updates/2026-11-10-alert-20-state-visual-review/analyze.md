# Analyze — Alert 20-state visual review

- The live playground exposes five tones, Soft/Glass surface, and Inline/Expanded layout, producing 20 combinations.
- Browser screenshots were reviewed for every combination. Soft surfaces use distinct status-tinted backgrounds; Glass surfaces stay white with tone-specific icons; Expanded retains title, description and the yellow-outlined action; Inline remains a single compact message. RTL keeps the icon at the right and close control at the left.
- No stretched alert geometry or clipping was visible in the reviewed desktop playground. Existing matched 499×932 frame measurements remain the source for the reference-size comparison.
- Independent screen-reader output has not been heard or verified and remains an open DoD requirement.
