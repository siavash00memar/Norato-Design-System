# Specification — Date Picker range selection color

## Goal

Align the interior of a selected date range with Norato's yellow accent palette.

## Acceptance

- Range interior uses a pale yellow semantic Norato token; endpoints remain brand yellow.
- No selection-state blue remains in the live range calendar.
- RTL, range value, disabled-day semantics and existing interaction geometry are unchanged.
- Browser evidence and token/catalog/type/build/diff checks are recorded; Date Picker DoD remains open for independent screen-reader review.
