# Spinner — Constitution

- Lemon is the authority for spinner shape families, tones, sizes and loader compositions; Norato keeps its own semantic palette and tokens.
- Spinner is status feedback, never a fake control. Preserve intrinsic dimensions and motion proportions; do not stretch the ring, dots or brand mark.
- RTL Persian content is the default. Loading copy is Vazirmatn; headings use Estedad.
- Reduced-motion preferences must be respected. A decorative spinner is hidden from assistive technology; its loader wrapper announces a localized status.
- Prefer semantic native Vue composition. No Reka primitive provides these visual variants, so CSS/SVG animation is appropriate.
