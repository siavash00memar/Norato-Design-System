# Toast — Constitution

- A Toast is short-lived post-action feedback; use Alert for important persistent errors and Banner for site-wide announcements.
- Keep RTL, Norato's own yellow brand accent, semantic status colors, Estedad headings, Vazirmatn body text and shared tokens.
- Follow Lemon composition: status icon at the inline end (right in RTL), message content, optional action, close at inline start (left), and a remaining-time bar; support Dark/Glass × Simple/Rich × Neutral/Success/Warning/Danger/Loading.
- Use the installed Reka UI Toast primitives when their provider/root/viewport/accessibility and timer behavior fit; do not rebuild announcement/timing semantics casually.
- A11y and comprehension outrank automatic disappearance: pause timing during pointer/focus interaction, never auto-dismiss Loading, allow a persistent duration, and keep action/close targets at least 48px.
- The specimen is inert; the playground is a separate real Vue interaction. Do not imply a queue, route, or action side effect the host did not provide.
- Do not close DoD before same-viewport Lemon/Norato comparison, all 20 combinations, behavior/RTL/accessibility, responsive widths and engineering gates are evidenced.
