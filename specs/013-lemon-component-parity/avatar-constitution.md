# Avatar — Constitution

- Build reusable Vue 3 avatars, groups, labels and presence status aligned with Lemon's composition while using Norato's own semantic palette and tokens.
- Persian and RTL are defaults. In RTL groups, the first avatar stays on the right and the `+N` counter sits on the left. The presence dot remains physically bottom-left as in Lemon.
- Never stretch profile images or glyphs; image content uses a square box with `object-fit: cover`. Keep the 2px surface ring and intrinsic dimensions consistent at every size.
- User names must remain available to assistive technology. Presence meaning must include text in the accessible name, not color alone.
- A group shows no more than five person avatars. Components remain presentation-only and do not fetch profile data.
- The component page follows Button: bounded inert preview, separate executable playground, copyable Vue usage, then the nine contract sections.
- Do not claim Avatar DoD complete until Lemon comparison, all 55 combinations, RTL, responsive geometry, keyboard/AX and engineering gates are evidenced.
